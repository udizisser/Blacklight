# Blacklight — product page draft (not for publishing)

A first draft for the website, kept here until stage 12 of `new-product` (drafts live only in this file). Every
number on the real page comes from the generated facts; nothing below has been measured or heard.

**Tagline:** Two knobs. A real space.

**Summary:** Blacklight is Clearlight's cinematic reverb engine in a closed box. Turn SIZE for how big the space is
and DECAY for how long it rings. Everything a room changes when it grows, its pre-delay, its early reflections, its
diffusion and how dark its tail gets, moves with those two knobs for you. Clean, wide and clear, on any track, in
seconds.

**At a glance (to fill from the facts):** two controls · the Clearlight engine · zero latency · stops costing CPU in
silence · twelve presets by use · Windows and macOS (Apple silicon), VST3, AU, AAX.

**Feature sections (drafts):**
- **Size moves the room, not just a number.** Bigger means a later first reflection, a wider early field, a denser
  tail and a more solid low end, the way real spaces grow.
- **Decay that stays pleasant.** Longer tails darken as they ring, so a long hall never turns into a hiss.
- **Clearlight inside.** The same engine that keeps a vocal clear inside a huge space, with its Clarity listening to
  your source.
- **A box that shows you the room.** A single glow: as wide as the space, as bright as the reverb, fading as the tail
  fades.

**Price:** the owner's (about $19-29). **Status:** beta until the owner releases it.
